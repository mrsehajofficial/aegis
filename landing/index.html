COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Aegis, who created it, and how it handles moderation.

Who created Aegis?

Aegis was designed and engineered by Sehaj Varma, an AI Automation Engineer & Backend Developer based in India. You can explore his other engineering work on his Portfolio or inspect the full source code on GitHub.

How does the anti-flood protection actually work?

Aegis tracks each user's message rate using in-memory sliding windows (by default, 5 messages in 3 seconds). If someone bursts messages faster than your threshold, Aegis immediately mutes or restricts them before spam overwhelms your chat.

What content types can I lock down?

You can lock 16 distinct message types: voice notes, animated stickers, external links, forwarded media, documents, inline bots, and more. Locks apply instantly to the group without needing to adjust Telegram's individual admin roles.

Is Aegis completely free and open source?

Yes, 100%. Aegis is released under the AGPL-3.0 open-source license. You can self-host it forever without paywalls, limits on group members, or premium tiers. The repository is on GitHub at github.com/mrsehajofficial/aegis.

How do I deploy Aegis in production?

You can launch the setup wizard with python -m app.setup which initializes your database and creates a secure environment file, or run it in Docker via docker compose up --build -d.

How does Aegis protect member privacy?

Aegis is completely self-hosted on your own infrastructure. Moderation logs and settings remain strictly inside your own database (SQLite or PostgreSQL). No analytics, chat messages, or user data are ever sent to third parties.
